INSTRUCTION
Each day, enter your BMR and NEAT values calculated above. You should only have to calculate this once and can reuse the same values each day as a baseline. You'll only need to recalculate if there's a change in weight and age.
Your BMR is important, because this is the absolute minimum amount of calories you need to consume on a daily basis for your vital organs to function. When you don't take in enough calories, your body starts to break down protein instead for fuel which can result in muscle loss.
If you're not taking in enough calories, your body triggers cortisol (stress hormone) and actually PROTECTS your bdoy fat because it thinks it needs to save it for emergency in order to keep vital organs functioning.
So if trying to lose body fat, aim for a Calorie Intake around +300 of your BMR - then aim for a deficit between 300-500.

TEF
TEF (thermic effect of food), are the calories your body burns to digest, absorb, and metabolize the food you eat. It generally acounts for 10% of your total calorie intake. This is automatically calculated for you into the deficit amount.
